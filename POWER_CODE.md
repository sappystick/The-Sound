## What, then, do the instructions "spell"?
**Not a hidden message.**
- No elemental, chemical, mathematical-constant, electrical or magnetic code appears beyond chance.
- The famous claims (π in the bronze sea, the ark as a capacitor) fail on their own terms.

**What they do encode, openly and consistently:**
1. **A measuring system:** cubits, decimal round numbers, simple ratios (1:1, 2:1, 3:2, 4:3, 5:3). In it, the rooms resonate as harmonic series (rounds 55–57).
2. **A geometry:** the cube at the centre (earth, in Plato's terms), squares around it, double squares as the approach.
3. **A map:** twelve around one, in a 4 × 3 grid, facing east. The camp, the breastpiece and the New Jerusalem's twelve gates share it.
4. **A material rule:** gold inside, silver and bronze outward, no iron. The holiest things are the least reactive metals, and the tools of war are kept out of the building ("no iron tool was heard").
5. **A centre:** the same "middle" structure found throughout this project. The seven-branched lamp has a central shaft, the camp a central tent, the book a central "I am" (rounds 50–54).

**In short:** the instructions spell out an ordered world in miniature, with a centre, four directions, twelve, seven, and the cube. That is a theological and cosmological design, written plainly. It is not a cipher.
