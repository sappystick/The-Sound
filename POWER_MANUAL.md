# Round 64: thought experiment, the Bible as the operating manual of the Great Pyramid

**The assumption, taken as true for this round:** the Bible is the manual for using the Great Pyramid.
- **The manual:** the one rite in which a single person enters the innermost chamber, the Day of Atonement (Lev 16; Mishnah Yoma; Tamid 3:8; all checked on Sefaria).
- **The method:** each step is placed in the pyramid (Mapping), and its physical effect there is calculated (Calc).

**Files:**
- script `power/round64_pyramid_manual.py`;
- numbers `power/round64_results.json`;
- audio **`power/audio/r64_kings_chamber_activation.mp3`**.

The divine name is not pronounced. The render uses the Mishnah's own substitution, *ana hashem* ("Please, O Name").

## What it would physically do
**1. The voice in the inner chamber.** The King's Chamber favours **132 Hz (C3)** and rings for about 6 s (granite).

| Voice | Level at 2 m |
|---|---|
| a chant | 77 dB |
| a loud voice | 87 dB |
| a shout | **94 dB**: past the ≈90 dB level at which loud low tones stimulate the balance organ |

A voice on the chamber's note would be **felt as well as heard** by the one inside.

**2. The chamber breathes.**
- The King's Chamber and its two shafts (about 0.20 × 0.20 m; about 71 m and 53 m long; approximate) form a **Helmholtz resonator** at **0.11 Hz**, one slow pulse every **9 s**.
- Wind across the shaft mouths could drive it.
- The render lets the chamber's note swell at this rate.
- Compare Noah's ark at 7 s (round 57).

**3. The voice carries out through the shafts:**

| Inside | 10 m from a shaft mouth | 50 m | 200 m |
|---|---|---|---|
| loud voice (87 dB) | 33 dB | 19 dB | 7 dB |
| shout (94 dB) | 40 dB | **26 dB** | 14 dB |

- With a night ambient of about 20 dB, **the inner voice is audible outside within about 50 m of the shaft mouths**.
- "Heard in Jericho" (Tamid 3:8) is not physically possible (round 47).
- **Reading under the experiment:** the people outside could hear the Name from the inner chamber and answer. That is step 9 of the manual.

**4. Do the ritual's numbers match the pyramid's?**
- **The pairs:**
  - 7 days and 7 sprinklings = the Gallery's 7 corbels;
  - 5 immersions = 5 relieving chambers;
  - 3 confessions = 3 plugs = 3 portcullises;
  - 4 linen garments = 4 air shafts;
  - 1 upward = 1 coffer.
- **Against chance:** 6 of the 9 ritual numbers occur among the pyramid's counts. Random small numbers give 5.4. **p = 0.49: no better than chance.** Small counts (1–10) always coincide.

## What the experiment shows
**Fits:**
- The rite has the right shape for the building: purification below, an ascent past barriers (3 plugs, then 3 portcullises), one person in the innermost chamber with a box, a cloud, measured strokes, and a voice.
- Physically, the King's Chamber would amplify and sustain a voice on its note, make it felt, and pass it faintly to listeners outside through the shafts.

**Does not fit:**
- **Orientation:** the tabernacle opens east and the priest walks west. The pyramid opens north.
- **Purpose:** the King's Chamber is a sealed tomb. The rite forbids the dead at the centre (Ezek 43:7; Lev 21:11; round 63).
- **The numbers** match only at chance level (p 0.49).

**Verdict:** the Day of Atonement can be "performed" in the pyramid as a coherent sequence, and the chamber would make its voice powerful and audible. But nothing in the texts points to the pyramid rather than to any inner sanctuary. The manual fits the tabernacle exactly and the pyramid only by analogy.
