# What The Sound has established: an evidence-graded synthesis (rounds 1–75; addendum at the end)

**Grades:**
- **A**: pre-registered and passed, or survives correction across the whole project.
- **B**: measured with controls; strong, but not pre-registered, or open to a known alternative.
- **C**: verifiable descriptive fact (anyone can check it in the text or a concordance).
- **D**: suggestive or exploratory.
- **N**: tested and negative (also a result).
- **S**: speculative construction (creative and educational; not a claim).

**Nothing here is "100%".** The A-grade results are the ones that would survive peer review as stated.

## B. Measured with controls (strong, not pre-registered)
| # | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Scribes corrected by ear.** Corrections are rarely sound-alike (P66 9.7%, Sinaiticus 1 of 26), while variants usually are (60%) | p ≈ 10⁻⁴⁰; Attic-pronunciation control ≈ 1%. Corrections and variants are different populations |
| B2 | **The papyri are laid out in spoken syllables:** 96–99% of line-end word splits fall at a syllable boundary (P66, P75, 01, 03), against 35% by chance | known scribal practice (K), quantified for John |
| B3 | **John was composed in Greek.** It carries more neighbour-word sound-patterning than the Syriac Peshitta | controls work: Hebrew Psalms beat their Greek translation |
| B4 | **The Aramaic words kept in the Gospels are Western/Palestinian:** attested in the targums, the Galilean Talmud and Qumran Aramaic, not the Syriac. *Abba* is absent from Qumran's literary Aramaic but frequent in Galilean | corpus counts across 6 corpora (partly K) |
| B5 | **Over half of manuscript variants sound identical in Koine** (52–60% in P66, 01, 03; P75 35%); about 0% under Attic | partial (the pre-registered rule failed on P75) |
| B6 | **John's chronology fits the lunar calendar** (0 contradictions against 4 for the 364-day calendar). The calendar engine reproduces 57/57 Qumran festival weekdays and 42/42 priestly courses | model comparison; the engine validated on the scrolls |
| B7 | **A crowd of 10–30 voices is acoustically "many waters"** (Ezek 43:2; Rev 1:15, 19:6) | spectral-flatness calculation |

## C. Verifiable facts newly assembled
- Coptic John translates the Word, life, light, truth and every "I am" predicate except "I am the resurrection". The single Sahidic word ⲙⲉ means both "truth" and "love" and is 4–11× denser in John.
- John's "opening week" (1:19–2:11) counts six days in the six early manuscripts checked. None has the reading that would supply a seventh.
- Notated Christian song: P.Oxy. 1786 writes sacred names in full under notes. P.Amh. 2 is accentual. Christian hymnody moved from vowel length to accent.
- **The seven-with-a-middle image** in sources of John's century: Philo (the menorah "imitates the choir of the seven planets … the middle of the seven, the sun", *Heres* 221–225); Josephus (*Ant.* 3.144–146; *War* 5.217); Revelation (the Son of Man "in the middle of the seven lampstands"); Pliny 2.84 (the Sun 4th of 7); Nicomachus (the mese = the Sun).
- **Temple sound practice:**
  - the ensemble minimums (Arakhin 2:3–6);
  - rhythm as ratios (Rosh Hashanah 4:9);
  - the liturgy's sound cues (Tamid 7:3);
  - cantillation hand signs (Berakhot 62a);
  - the *kinnor* of 10 strings and the *nabla* of 12 notes (Josephus, *Ant.* 7.306);
  - silver trumpets under a cubit, with a bell (*Ant.* 3.291);
  - 72 bells (Zevachim 88b).
- **Acoustic facts of the stated dimensions:**
  - the ark sounds 2 octaves above the tabernacle's inner room and 3 above Solomon's;
  - the tabernacle, Solomon's house and Noah's ark resonate as single harmonic series;
  - Solomon's side chambers are 5, 6, 7 cubits (1 Kgs 6:6).
- Heron's *Pneumatica* (1st c.) documents temple sound machines: a door-opening trumpet, singing birds, a hissing altar.

## D. Suggestive (open)
- **The solemn sayings carry an Aramaic sound-layer:** John p 0.20; Matthew p 0.044 (secondary).
- **Vision chapters crescendo:** p 0.0014, exploratory; **did not replicate** (N).
- **"I am" verses cadence on the mese under the accent melody:** passes in the LXX, but grammatical.

## N. Negative results (these are contributions too)
No hidden number codes, no letter skip-codes, no Sefer Yetzirah letter power, no melody in the letters, no section modes, no string-ratio timing, and no "Amen" cues at the joints. Thomas does not share John's vocabulary. Sacred-name abbreviations are not a theology code. Original wording is not acoustically stronger than translation. Sound cannot topple walls.

## S. Speculative constructions
- **The instrument model:** the tabernacle and temples as one resonator; the ark zither; the speaking planet organ.
- **What is real:** the calculated values are real, and the design is buildable.
- **What is not shown:** no text describes these objects as one instrument.
- **Where it belongs:** in exhibitions, music and teaching, not in a historical claim.

## Threads that can be closed with a little more work
1. **Sensitivity of the ring (A2) to the saying list.** Rerun with every alternative occurrence (6:41/48/51; 9:5; 10:9; 10:14; 15:5) and report the share of variants that pass.
2. **An independent replication.** A second analyst reruns rounds 52–54 from the pre-registrations alone.
3. **Hand-check 50 scribal corrections (B1),** and extend the test to P75 and P46.
4. **A pre-registered sayings-language test** on all four Gospels: Jesus' words against narration, Greek against Syriac.
5. **Verify the outlines** (Brown, Moloney, Dodd) against the printed books, the open limit of A1.
6. **Blind listening tests:** E5 (original against translated against scrambled), and whether listeners can identify the planet organ's vowels.
7. **Ekphonetic lectionaries:** whether sacred names attract lengthening signs. Needs one digitised lectionary.

## Limits that apply to everything
- 59 rounds: many paths were explored before pre-registration began, so even the A results should be read with that history in mind.
- The pronunciation, the reconstructed Aramaic and Egyptian vowels, and the synthetic voices are models.
- No outside expert has reviewed the work yet.
