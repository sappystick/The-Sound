# Timeline tree: the historical Johns

Status: **checked** = read in the Greek here (code/analysis/john_history.py) · **trad** = tradition · **arch** = archaeology

## Trunk: the era
- 20/19 BC: Herod begins rebuilding the temple (Josephus, Antiquities 15.380)
- c. 7–4 BC: births of John the Baptist and Jesus (Luke 1–2; Herod died 4 BC)
- AD 18–36: Caiaphas high priest (**arch**: family ossuary, 1990) · AD 26–36: Pilate (**arch**: Caesarea inscription, 1961)

## Branch 1: John the Baptist
- AD 27–29: preaching at the Jordan. John 2:20 gives AD 27/28; Luke 3:1 gives AD 28/29. The two agree.
- Bethany beyond the Jordan (John 1:28); Aenon near Salim (3:23). Both are on the Madaba mosaic map (6th century; **arch**).
- Imprisoned and killed at Machaerus (Josephus, Antiquities 18.116–119, **checked**; **arch**: excavations from 2009). Herod's defeat by Aretas IV in AD 36 was seen as punishment.
- His movement continued: disciples at Ephesus (Acts 19:1–7), Apollos of Alexandria (Acts 18:24), Pseudo-Clementine Recognitions 1.60, the Mandaeans today.
- Possible link with Qumran: Isaiah 40:3 in 1QS 8:14; washing; "sons of light" (open).

## Branch 2: John son of Zebedee
- Fisherman, one of the Twelve, in the inner circle (Mark 1:19; 5:37; 9:2). A "pillar" (Galatians 2:9, c. AD 48). Early martyrdom tradition (Mark 10:39; Philip of Side; disputed).

## Branch 3: the disciple whom Jesus loved
- John 13:23; 19:26; 20:2; 21:7, 20, 24 (he "wrote these things"). "Known to the high priest" (18:15, if he is the "other disciple").

## Branch 4: John the Elder, at Ephesus
- Papias (c. 110–130): "the elder John", listed separately from the apostle; two tombs at Ephesus (Eusebius 3.39, **checked**)
- Polycrates (c. 190): John "who became a priest wearing the *petalon*", buried at Ephesus (Eusebius 3.31.3, **checked**)
- Irenaeus (c. 180): the Gospel was published at Ephesus; John lived until Trajan (98–117). Clement (c. 200): a "spiritual gospel" (Eusebius 6.14.7, **checked**)
- Patmos under Domitian (Eusebius 3.18, **checked**; Revelation 1:9)

## Branch 5: the writings
- Gospel c. AD 90–110; P52 (c. 125–175), P66 and P75 (c. 175–225). 1–3 John ("the elder"). Revelation.
- Crucifixion on Nisan 14, a Friday: 7 April AD 30 or 3 April AD 33.
