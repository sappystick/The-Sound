The Bible arranged by shape, not by sequence

Each dot is one chapter of the Greek Bible (Septuagint + New Testament; 1,355 chapters of 100+ words). Every chapter was measured on its own: vowel colour, breath, pace, mirror symmetry, word-staircases, and its share of light, voice, sight, breath and water words. Chapters that sound and are built alike sit close together. Book order plays no part in the layout.

Does the symmetry measure work?

The two chapters with the strongest mirror symmetry in the whole Bible are:

  • Psalm 104 (103 Greek): the creation hymn
  • Psalm 8: opens and closes with "O Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth"

Psalm 8's ring is long recognised, so the measure finds real rings. The strongest staircases are genealogies such as Matthew 1, as expected.

The Wisdom–Word thread

One family (77 chapters) gathers the main "light" texts from every part of the Bible: Genesis 1, Wisdom 7, Sirach 24, Baruch 3, John 1, 1 John 1, Revelation 21 and 22. It runs from creation to the new city, through the Wisdom poems John's prologue draws on. The nearest chapters in shape include 2 Corinthians 4 ("light shine out of darkness"), Psalm 36 ("in your light we see light"), Matthew 4, Acts 26, John 3 and John 12.

One of them, found by shape alone, is Wisdom 18:14–15:

ἡσύχου γὰρ σιγῆς περιεχούσης τὰ πάντα καὶ νυκτὸς ἐν ἰδίῳ τάχει μεσαζούσης ὁ παντοδύναμός σου λόγος ἀπ᾽ οὐρανῶν … ἥλατο
“While gentle silence held all things, and night in its swift course was at its midpoint, your all-powerful Word leaped from heaven.”

The Word arrives at the midpoint of the night. That is the image this study measured in John's prologue, whose spoken centre holds in every manuscript tested. The link is suggestive, not proven. Wisdom 18 sits at 19:14–15 in the Swete file's numbering.
