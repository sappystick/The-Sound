Formula by formula

What is being tested

Origen wrote that an incantation "spoken in its own language achieves what it promises; but translated into any other tongue it becomes slack and powerless. So it is not the meanings of things but the qualities of the sounds that have power." Iamblichus said the same of sacred names.

Each of eight formulas is heard in three versions:

Main comparison: original against scrambled. Both are spoken by the same voice from the same syllables, so any difference comes from the specific arrangement of sounds, not from foreignness. Second comparison: original against translation, which uses a different voice and so should be read with care.

Voices: the original and scrambled versions use a neural voice speaking reconstructed first-century Koine pronunciation, shifted to an adult male range; the translations use a male English neural voice. All are matched for loudness and placed in the same small hall.
