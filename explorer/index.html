Findings and their audit status

The audit was pre-registered: outlines, settings and pass/fail rules were committed before any robustness test ran. Statuses come from that run.

Does the text draw its own outline?

Each dot is one setting: an outline × a minimum section length × a definition of the voice signal, for k = 4 sections. Further right means the blind cuts land closer to that outline than random cuts do. Hollow rings are the baseline settings. The dashed line is p = 0.05.

Variants that sound the same

Under Attic pronunciation almost every variant changes the sound; under Koine about half do not. The scribes wrote what they heard. P75 has only 86 variants, which is why it falls short of the 50% rule.

Multiple testing across the whole project

How the spoken reconstruction is made

Text: SBLGNT. Pronunciation: Koine Greek of about the first century (vowel mergers, aspirates as stops), from the project's phonological engine. Delivery: public reading. Room: a synagogue hall, reverberation time 1.4 s. Pauses: 0.6 s between verses; 3 s before the three major self-found section breaks (1:16, 13:33, 18:1); 2 s before the minor ones. The voice is a formant synthesiser: it renders sounds, stress, pitch and the room, not a human performer.

Reproduce it
