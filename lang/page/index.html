In numbers

Fifty syllables

Ten consonants and five vowels, chosen because most of the world's languages already have them. Each syllable has a sign and a number (consonant × 5 + vowel). In the sign, the stroke is the consonant and the dot's position is the vowel: below a, right e, above i, centre o, left u. Lip sounds are round, tongue-tip sounds are straight, back sounds are angled.

The hand: a sign for every syllable

The handshape is the consonant; where it is held is the vowel. A root is two quick signs. This layer is a proposal and has not been tried with signers.
