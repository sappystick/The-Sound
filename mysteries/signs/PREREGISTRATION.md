# Pre-registration: is a "Signs Gospel" source detectable in John's style? (written before the analysis script)

**Question.** Bultmann (1941) and Fortna (1970) argue that John's miracle stories come from a written Signs Source. Ruckstuhl (1951) and Schweizer argue that John's stylistic traits are spread evenly, so no source can be told apart by style.

**Source passages (fixed in advance).** These are the seven sign narratives plus the catch of fish, as in Fortna's reconstruction:
- 2:1-11
- 4:46-54
- 5:2-9
- 6:1-14
- 6:16-21
- 9:1-7
- 11:1-44
- 21:1-14

**Comparison.** All other passages of John, excluding the prologue 1:1-18. Genre is controlled: each source passage is compared with random contiguous blocks of non-source verses of the *same length*, whose share of narrative verses (verses with no 1st- or 2nd-person forms) is within ±0.15 of the source passage's own share.

**Features** (rates per 100 words, SBLGNT):
1. οὖν
2. δέ
3. καί
4. asyndetic verses (no conjunction among the first three words)
5. historic present λέγει / λέγουσιν
6. ἐκεῖνος
7. ἵνα
8. ὁ Ἰησοῦς (article + Jesus)
9. μετὰ τοῦτο / ταῦτα
10. πάλιν
11. Ἰουδαῖοι
12. mean words per verse

**Statistic.** For each feature, the difference between the source passages (pooled) and the matched comparison, standardised by the spread of that difference over 5,000 matched random draws. Omnibus statistic = the sum of squared z-scores. p = the share of draws (with random "source" sets drawn the same way) at least as extreme.

**Power check (must pass for a null to mean anything).** Replace the eight source passages with eight *Mark* passages of the same lengths (Mark 1:29-31, 1:40-45, 2:1-12, 4:35-41, 5:21-43, 6:32-44, 6:45-52, 8:22-26: Mark's miracle stories), compared with the same John comparison pool. If the method does not separate Mark from John (p < 0.01), it has no power, and any null for the Signs Source is uninformative.

**Decision rule.**
- **Source detected:** omnibus p < 0.01 AND the power check passes. Individual features are reported after Benjamini–Hochberg correction.
- **Ruckstuhl supported:** omnibus p ≥ 0.05 AND the power check passes.
- **Otherwise:** inconclusive.

**Sensitivity analysis:** the same test without 21:1-14, because many scholars take chapter 21 to be an appendix.
