# Pre-registration 2: is "ὁ Ἰησοῦς" (Jesus with the article) a marker of the Signs Source? (written before the script)

**Background.** Round 74's pre-registered test found the Signs Source passages stylistically distinct (p = 0.0004). The feature not explained by genre was "ὁ Ἰησοῦς". That finding came from those passages, so re-testing them is not a replication. The new evidence must come from **other passages** and **other authors**.

**Measure.** For each occurrence of Ἰησοῦς (any case), "articular" means the definite article in the same case stands immediately before it, or one word before it with a postpositive particle in between (δέ, οὖν, γάρ, μέν, τε). The statistic is the **share of Ἰησοῦς occurrences that are articular**, which does not depend on how often Jesus is named. Significance comes from 10,000 matched random blocks (same lengths, from the comparison pool), as in round 74.

**T1, refined measure on the core passages (descriptive, not new evidence).** The 8 core Signs passages of round 74 against the rest of John (excluding 1:1-18).

**T2, extension to the wider source (the decisive test inside John).** Further passages Fortna (1970) assigns to the Signs Gospel. This approximate list is fixed now and rounded to whole pericopes:
- 1:19-49 (John the Baptist, first disciples)
- 2:14-19 (temple action)
- 4:4-42 (the Samaritan woman)
- 12:1-15 (anointing, entry)
- 18:1-19:42 (the passion narrative)
- 20:1-20 (the empty tomb and appearances)

These are compared with the rest of John minus all source passages, core and extended.
**Prediction if "ὁ Ἰησοῦς" marks the source:** the articular share is higher in the extended passages too (one-sided, p < 0.05).

**T3, genre control in the other Gospels.** In Matthew, Mark and Luke, the articular share in miracle stories against the rest of the same Gospel:
- **Matthew:** 8:1-4, 8:5-13, 8:14-15, 8:23-27, 8:28-34, 9:1-8, 9:18-26, 9:27-31, 14:13-21, 14:22-33, 15:32-39, 20:29-34.
- **Mark:** 1:29-31, 1:40-45, 2:1-12, 4:35-41, 5:1-20, 5:21-43, 6:32-44, 6:45-52, 8:1-10, 8:22-26, 10:46-52.
- **Luke:** 4:38-39, 5:12-16, 5:17-26, 7:1-10, 7:11-17, 8:22-25, 8:40-56, 9:10-17, 13:10-17, 18:35-43.

**Decision rule.**
- **SOURCE MARKER SUPPORTED:** T2 is significant in the predicted direction (p < 0.05), AND fewer than 2 of the 3 Synoptics show a significant (p < 0.05) higher articular share in their miracle stories.
- **GENRE EXPLANATION:** at least 2 of the 3 Synoptics show that.
- **Otherwise:** NOT SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE, reported as such.
