# Pre-registration 3: do the sign stories carry the evangelist's own style? (round 79)

Written and committed **before** the analysis script `ruckstuhl.py` exists.

## Question
Ruckstuhl (1951) and Schweizer (1939) answered source theories of John with lists of the evangelist's characteristic
expressions, found all through the Gospel. Round 74 showed the core sign stories differ from John's other narrative
(p = 0.0004), but round 74b showed its most specific marker does not carry over. This test asks the opposite question,
with the criteria the unity side itself proposed: **are the evangelist's characteristics thinner in the sign stories?**
A separate written source should carry fewer of them; the evangelist writing freely should carry the normal share.

## Characteristics counted (lemma-based on SBLGNT, fixed now)
1. ἐκεῖνος  2. ἵνα  3. οὖν  4. ἐμός  5. ἴδε  6. ἀμὴν ἀμήν  7. καθώς  8. ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν (ἀποκρίνομαι followed
within 3 words by καί and λέγω)  9. ἀλλ' ἵνα (ἀλλά followed within 2 words by ἵνα)  10. τότε οὖν  11. partitive ἐκ
(ἐκ followed by a genitive plural article).
All are on the Ruckstuhl / Schweizer lists or the summaries of them (ἐκεῖνος, ἵνα epexegetic, οὐ … ἀλλ' ἵνα, οὖν
historicum, τότε οὖν, καθώς … καί, partitive ἐκ, ἐμός, ἴδε, ἀμὴν ἀμήν, ἀπεκρίθη καὶ εἶπεν). Word-level counts cannot
separate the epexegetic ἵνα or the historic οὖν from other uses; the totals are used.

## Statistic and null
- **Primary:** the composite rate (all 11 counted together, per 100 words) in the round-74 core sign passages.
- **Null:** 5,000 sets of matched blocks from John outside the passages and after 1:18, same lengths, narrative share
  within 0.15 (identical to round 74).
- **One-sided:** source lower than the null.

## Power check (must pass, p < 0.01 one-sided)
Mark's eight miracle stories (as round 74) against the same John null. A text by another author should show far fewer
Johannine characteristics.

## Decision
- p < 0.05 and power passes: **CHARACTERISTICS THINNED**: supports a separate source for the sign stories.
- p ≥ 0.05 and power passes: **EVANGELIST'S STYLE PRESENT**: the sign stories carry the evangelist's characteristics at
  the normal rate (Ruckstuhl's position for this material).
- Power fails: inconclusive.
Per-characteristic rates are reported descriptively only.
